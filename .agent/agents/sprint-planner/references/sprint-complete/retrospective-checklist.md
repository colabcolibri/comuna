# Retrospective — fill at `/complete-sprint`

> Process, not product. Sprint **review** (increment vs `goal`) is the **human**. This section is how the harness and the team worked.

## Gate

`update-sprint` with `status: complete` **fails** unless this section is filled. Placeholder `_(fill at sprint close)_` is invalid.

## Required headings

### What worked

At least one bullet with a **fact**: US Record path, validator behavior, station bounce, gate that caught a mistake.

### What to improve

At least one bullet: what to change **next sprint** (protocol, station, close habit). Not “try harder”.

### Decisions to log

`_n/a_` or items that must go to `prepend-decision` (cross-cutting). If you list a decision here, run `prepend-decision` in the same turn.

## Forbidden

- Empty `-` bullets under the three headings
- Copying the template labels with no prose
- Inventing a retro so hygiene stops warning
- Closing while any linked US is not `✅` / `🚫` / `🧊`

## Review vs retro

| | Review | Retrospective |

| When | Before `complete` | Same `/complete-sprint` turn, after review |
